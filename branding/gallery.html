Making a new one

Pip is stored as layers: body, pose, face, things to wear and background scenes. A version is a few lines in presets.toml, or a one-off command.

# presets.toml
[events.christmas]
face  = "happy"
wear  = ["hat-santa"]
scene = "snow"
bg    = "#1F7A5C"
./build.py --png 512     # rebuild everything, this page included
./build.py base --bg '#0EA5E9' --shape circle -o pip-blue.svg

For a new event, draw the hat or prop over pip-base-plain, save it into parts/wear/ and add a preset. The steps are in README.md.
